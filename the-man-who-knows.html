the man who knows

2024

film

To die young on his Harley with the wind in his hair was all Alexander wanted. Back in the seventies, he was part of a rocker gang. Yet, life does not always work out as planned. Forty years later, Alexander still lives in the same town in Bavaria, working as a spiritual medium, fortune teller, vodoo priest and exorcist.
